# Flow organism revision

The current brief supersedes the mask family artistic decisions. Implementation is
inline, with one final whole-change review; routine design choices are delegated.

## Architecture
Use a bounded polar-field family of variable-width curved ribbons. Primary ribbons
wrap around procedurally placed focal regions and continue into rounded appendages.
Each ribbon is a distinct anatomical plate at its own relief level, rather than a
copy of a common central outline. Elliptic/whorled and staggered bilateral fields
share the same ribbon primitive; focal count, phase and spacing are composition.
Complexity adds nested secondary lobes, segmentation and tertiary channels without
changing primary paths or focal positions. Size fits the entire composition in XY
before adding fixed-size joinery. Depth sets actual plate elevations independently.

Keep old creature configs as legacy. Add an `organism` configuration group with
composition, complexity and style fields; envelope remains requested size in mm.
Initial target: 800–1400 mm width/height, aspect ratio 0.7–1.4, depth 120–240 mm.
Only successful generated and validated combinations are claimed as demonstrated.

## Construction choice
Each lobe is a coherent module: one projection plate, two substantial upright
plywood trestles with closed locating slots, and two ordinary through-bolt clamps.
Trestle shoulders carry the plate; clamping bolts secure it to a shaped plywood
backing. No plywood spacer rings, individual rib keys or positive clips. Detached
modules require a supporting cradle until bolts are secured. Purchased bolt length,
stock fit and structural capacity require physical selection/testing.

Alternative repeated stacked shells preserve the dominant slab problem. Tubular
spacers reduce cut count but add custom lengths and loose hardware. Trestles create
actual depth and retain an ordinary, inspectable load path with few cut parts.

## Validation
Check connected contours, morphological neck width plus explicit hole/slot margins,
neighboring slots, backing bearing, fitted tab/slot dimensions, actual solids and
hardware corridors, envelope and sampled ordered removal. Preserve identified CAD,
SVG/GLB/JSON, surface atlas, ISF/Wire resources and existing rotatable preview.
Inventory signature includes all manufacturing controls; reconfiguration may only
reuse identical manufactured geometry. Physical and host checks remain NOT RUN.

Coarse studies precede joints. Inspect actual final front/oblique/side CAD as well.
Aesthetic assessment is distinct from numerical validity.

Implementation decision: high complexity adds integral divisions and channels after
medium adds inner lobes. A second small lobe tier was rejected because its support
patches crowded other modules and its visible return did not justify extra parts.
The backing uses 28 mm rails, not eroded copies of front plates: the latter left
3–5 mm hidden ligaments despite valid-looking contours. Exact cut distances gate it.
