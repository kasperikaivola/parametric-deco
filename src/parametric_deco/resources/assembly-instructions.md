# Prototype only — physical tests NOT RUN

1. Measure plywood. Edit measured_mm and measurement_status, cut the fit coupon
   with kerf compensation in laser software only; verify 100 mm scale marks.
2. Use the generated manifest for counts, revisions and instance IDs. Dry-assemble
   paired spines on a flat support tray; lower end ribs, then interior ribs into
   their numbered stations. Slot datum is common to all compatible ribs.
3. Lower the real slotted upper closure onto the rib tops and end tongues. Check
   contact at every rib. Do not substitute a short cap at the root.
4. Keep closure and bottom tray compressed together with a hand/helper. Seat on
   backing, align both labelled grid positions and insert long root through-bolts,
   washers and accessible rear nuts. Hardware envelope is illustrative only.
5. Inspect fit, root stiffness, tool access, retention and alignment before use.
6. To detach: support tray FIRST; keep sandwich compressed; remove both bolts;
   follow reported module extraction direction/order. Move supported to bench.
7. Lift closure clear of the end tongues, then lift ribs along +Z. Rearrange
   identified ribs only at compatible manufactured stations. Rebuild and remap.

Never lift an unbolted module by the closure. No independent detached locking,
structural load rating or automatic approval for installation is claimed.
